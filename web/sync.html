Position — ENU in time

Every 5-min snapshot fix, converted to ENU metres relative to the surveyed site, last 3 h. teal = East, amber = North, blue = Up. Filled markers = gated (redundant) fixes; open = ungated exact solves (unverifiable — honesty stays visible); purple edge = mixed GPS+BDS solve. The y axis is robust: median ± 8×MAD per component, so a bad fix is marked at the edge instead of flattening the rest. The small strip under the chart is the mixed solve's GPS−BDS inter-system clock (isx_km) — its stability is the anti-spoof observable.
